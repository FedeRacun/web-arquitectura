## Scope

The one-page site (`src/pages/index.astro`), visitor mode: Experience with a Persuade close (the WhatsApp action in scene 8).

## Audience and job

Prospective clients of an architect who designs and builds, deciding whom to trust with their next building. Generic demo: no studio name, no projects, no proof beyond the procedural building itself.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The visitor builds the building with their own scroll, in one continuous WebGL scene that goes line, mass, matter, light, structure. It refuses the category default: a render gallery, a project grid and a hero photo with a contact form.

OWN-WORLD: Warm black #141210 under a concrete grain; bone #EDE6DA for type and for charcoal linework that trembles like a Kahn sketch; copper #B8743A only for the cursor, the button and one word per scene. DM Serif Display sentences set large with air. Hairline leader lines like axonometric callouts. A CAD crosshair cursor. Ando concrete with formwork tie holes, Zumthor hard light, a balsa model under a single lamp.

STORY: The visitor understands that design comes before building, believes this architect carries a line all the way to a finished volume, and ends on "Tu próxima obra." with a single WhatsApp button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed dark void with grain. A point of light at the centre slides to a corner and traces the site's rectangle in perspective in a bone line. Then "Primero se piensa. Después se construye." in DM Serif, low and centred, "construye" in copper, and a plumb-line scroll hint at the bottom centre. No nav, no logo, no action until scene 8.

FORM: The brief's eight-scene storyboard, pinned by the brief; no concept-seed roll (a brief-pinned direction beats the roll); code-led, no image generation available. Signature interaction: the scroll draws, and stopping leaves the stroke halfway. Motion grammar: slow, heavy, decelerating; nothing bounces.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
